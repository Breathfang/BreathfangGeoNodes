# Collaborators

Contributors who maintain DragonGraph's Toolset Pack alongside the lead maintainer. Their
nodes are merged into the main pack file and live under the `Collabs` category in Blender
Assets.

## Maintainers

| Handle | Role |
| ------ | ---- |
| Breathfang | Lead maintainer. Reviews and merges contributions. |
| TheLycanFenrir | Maintainer. |

More maintainers will be listed here as they are added.

## Adding a maintainer

Maintainers are added by request. Please [open an issue](https://github.com/Breathfang/BreathfangGeoNodes/issues/)
and confirm that you have read the [contribution requirements](../../CONTRIBUTING.md) first.

Once approved, add your handle to the table above.
